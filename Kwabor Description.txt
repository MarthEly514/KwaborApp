So app name: Kwabor
Description: its a digital invitation app that allows its users to create events and share their invitation links. These links redirect to a custom page where the receiver gets a custom invitation card, will you come: yes/no, etc.

Functionalities: on top of the ones of this https://www.invitiz.app/dashboard app, add: 
- agenda reminder
- a "J'y serai" numerical badge generation for the ones who accept to attend the event
- a customizable color palette (just predefined colors, not a color wheel/ color picker to not loose the user)
- custom invitations for some specific users (The one sending the invite select/enters specific informations about some of the invited ones and for each of them, so they will get a personalized link with their names on the invite card.)
- whatsapp reminder (the user to confirm his place enters his whatsapp or email (optional))
- QR code ticket for paid events (live concerts, etc..)
- payment mode (mobile money by default but also credit card for foreigners)
- the ability for the user (event creator) to add photos of the event to the app and after the event, a link is generated and sent to all the confirmed participants. (conservation limit == 30 days max (also customizable by the user (from 1 week to 30 days))).
- google maps location to guide the invited

Stack: 
* react js x Typescript
* Tailwind css
* Material icons
* Gsap for fluid animations
* lenis for clean scroll

Use good UI/UX principles from your skills to build the app and just get the inspiration (pages, functionalities) from the invitz app, not their style
